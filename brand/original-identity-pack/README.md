# Assay — Fold identity system

## Direction
**Fold / Refine**: three offset planes create a layered, folded mark. It is intended to bridge Scenes and creative composition with the evidence-led discipline of Lab.

## Files
- `svg/`: symbol, wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups, monochrome and module-colour marks.
- `svg/glyphs/`: 14 original 24 px outline glyphs (five tabs and nine tools).
- `icons/AppIcon.appiconset/`: primary 1024 px icon and Xcode asset catalog.
- `icons/AlternateIcons/`: Dark, Tinted, Pinstripe and XP alternate icon art/catalogs.
- `preview/`: theme and small-size review sheets.
- `Assay-Fold-Identity-Summary.pdf`: one-page overview.

## Symbol construction and use
- Clear space: minimum one symbol x-height around the outer silhouette.
- Preferred minimum: 24 px; 16 px only for simplified use and after optical testing.
- Use solid black on warm pastel surfaces, warm white on dark, and a solid mark on frosted glass.
- Pinstripe: white mark on dark glossy inset. XP: white mark on saturated blue with a restrained top gloss.
- Do not skew, rotate, outline, add bevels to the core mark, or separate the planes into unrelated colours.
- The glyph family uses 1.9 px strokes at a 24 px viewBox, round caps and joins.

## Motion (900 ms)
1. 0–180 ms: upper plane enters from 8 px above; spring response 0.82, damping 0.72.
2. 180–420 ms: centre plane rises and seats; seam settles to 2 px.
3. 420–650 ms: lower plane folds upward with 1.04x overshoot.
4. 650–900 ms: one restrained highlight travels across the seam; mark settles.

## Type
The wordmark SVG uses a geometric sans stack (Inter / Arial) as editable text, not outlined paths. For production, replace with a licensed, custom-tuned geometric grotesk and convert to outlines in the final master.
