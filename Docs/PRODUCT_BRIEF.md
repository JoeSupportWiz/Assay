# Product brief

**Working name: Assay.** Not trademark/App Store cleared. The approved brand direction and screen designs are in `../brand/`, `../design/`, `../docs/screens/` and `../prototype/`.

## Promise

A polished, trustworthy iPhone customization and developer studio that unifies supported device tweaks, Scenes, wallpapers/`.tendies`, Wallet visuals, app signing, diagnostics, BLE/LAN tools and AI-assisted creation. It shows what it knows, what it changes, its risks and how to recover.

## Approved feature vision

Home Screen Scenes pairing wallpaper, user-approved app/folder layout, widgets and Shortcuts; device passport and diagnostics; compatibility radar; evidence-labelled issue diagnosis and repair; safety-tiered tweak catalog/custom builder; wallpaper/.tendies editor; AI workshop (Minis where supported, hosted providers, Ollama); per-target app/appex signing and developer account guidance; visual-only Wallet card art; widgets, Live Activities, Control Center and Shortcuts; BLE Inspector-inspired authorized diagnostics; local Bonjour/LAN inventory; pairing/loopback tools; backup health/cache/recovery; crash/log triage; updates, privacy controls and operation receipts. Full 38-feature backlog and platform constraints are in the attached design/product handoff and Assay Design Bundle.

## Technical truth

Every capability is gated by iOS build, device, entitlement, account permissions and APIs. A design mockup is not proof of platform support. Preserve the product rules: preflight → snapshot where possible → diff → explicit confirmation → apply → verify where possible → receipt → rollback path. Do not claim success without a verification signal. Live Activities are display surfaces, not background execution entitlements. Do not enable location as a keep-alive. Signing renewals and permission changes must be owner-authorized. Network/BLE operations are visible and authorized. AI drafts/proposes; user approves consequential actions.
