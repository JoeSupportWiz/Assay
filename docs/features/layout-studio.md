# Home Screen layout planner

**Entry:** Present `SceneLayoutPlannerView()` inside an existing iOS 17 `NavigationStack`. The feature does not create its own stack or add a tab or route to the host.

Name a page, optionally add its purpose, then type app labels one per line. Blank lines are ignored; duplicates and input order are preserved. Choose the part of the wallpaper to keep clear and reserve space for at most one widget. The 4-column, 6-row sketch leaves rows 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6 clear. A small (2×2) or medium (4×2) reservation starts at the left of the first free two-row band. Apps fill remaining cells from left to right, top to bottom. That leaves room for 16, 12 or 8 labels with no, small or medium widget respectively. Extra labels appear under **Unplaced** and are saved, not moved to another page. Each grid cell has a spoken row, column and role. **App positions** lists the full placed labels.

**Save and restore:** Pick a scene from Assay’s saved scene library or leave **No scene** selected for a standalone named recipe. The active scene is never selected implicitly. **Save layout** adds a snapshot, newest first, in the app’s local `Application Support/LayoutStudio/plans-v1.json`. There is no automatic draft save. Open any saved layout to review it; **Restore as draft → Replace draft** restores its inputs without overwriting any saved copy. Scene associations use UUIDs, so renames do not break them. A missing scene is marked unavailable; explicitly choose a different scene or **No scene** before saving a new copy. Unreadable history is not replaced.

**Limits:** This is a manual arrangement guide, not an installer or a device-accurate rendering. Wallpaper subject and widget reservations are user choices; the purpose is a page note, not an automatic app or colour classifier. No enumeration of installed apps, wallpaper analysis, Home Screen reads, scene activation, system edits, private APIs, sync or permissions. The dock, folders, other grid sizes and OS-specific placement constraints are not modelled.

**Checks:** `sh tests/layout-studio/smoke.sh` runs the Foundation-only Swift tests. For a UI check, enter 17 labels, choose top/medium and verify 9 unplaced, then save both a standalone copy and a copy linked to a non-active scene. After relaunch, review and restore the earlier version; verify the scene and system appearance never change. With VoiceOver, inspect a clear cell, widget cell, app, unused cell on a sparse page, and an unplaced label.
