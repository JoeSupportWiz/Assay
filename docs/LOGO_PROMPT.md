# Logo & app icon brief: hand this to a logo/identity agent

Copy everything below the line into the other agent. Attach `docs/screens/*.png` and `docs/showcase/*.png` from this bundle as visual context.

---

You are a senior brand identity designer. Design the **logo system and iOS app icon** for an iPhone app with the working name **"Assay"**. The name isn't final, so build the system so it still works if the name changes. Deliver original work only.

## The product (what the mark has to carry)

Assay is a polished, trustworthy iPhone **customisation studio and developer/device lab** in one app. Its core experience is **Scenes**: a saved set of wallpaper, Home Screen layout, widgets, Shortcuts and a Focus label (Work, Sleep, Travel, Research…) that you preview, apply step by step and can restore.

Navigation is **Home · Scenes · Studio · Lab · More**.

- **Studio** holds the creative tools:
  - device tweaks (Gild)
  - wallpapers and `.tendies` projects (Canvas)
  - Wallet card visuals, visual only (Cards)
  - AI-assisted asset creation (Alchemy)
- **Lab** holds the technical tools:
  - diagnostics with evidence, plus crash and log explainers (Device check)
  - the signing matrix, entitlement diff and build receipts (Signing)
  - a Bluetooth investigation notebook
  - network inventory
  - pairing and tunnel health
  - backups with receipts and rollback (Vault)

So the identity must stretch across **creative** work (scenes, wallpapers, art) and **serious** work (signing, diagnostics) without looking frivolous or like a terminal.

Its promise is **"Shows its work."** Every change is previewed, explained, applied one step at a time, verified and can be undone.

The audience is technically capable iPhone owners, Apple developers and security/networking practitioners. They care about Apple-quality craft and are also creative: they make wallpapers and themes. The brand has to feel **credible to a security researcher and delightful to a creative user** at the same time.

"Assay" means testing metal to find out what it really contains. The ideas behind it are inspection, proof, honesty, refinement and craft. The modules use a light metalwork vocabulary (Gild, Signet, Vault, Alchemy). Use that as a reference point if it helps, but **do not default to a literal gold nugget, ingot, gem or pickaxe.** A faceted gold ingot was already tried and rejected.

## The UI the logo must sit inside (already designed and approved)

- **Look:** a light, playful, premium iOS app in the style of top Dribbble/Instagram mobile UI. Warm off-white background `#F3F2EE`, white cards with 28 px radius, black pill buttons `#111113`, chunky Inter Display headings with tight tracking (−3%).
- **Pastel module colours:**
  - Lilac `#DCD1FF` (accent `#7C5CFF`)
  - Sky `#C4E6FF` (`#2E9BFF`)
  - Mint `#BDF3D7` (`#38C98A`)
  - Butter `#FFE58A` (`#E9C24A`)
  - Peach `#FFD6BF` (`#FF7A3D`)
- **Icon tiles:** glossy gradient squircles with an inner top highlight and a coloured drop shadow.
- **Motion:** springy and confident. Cards rise in staggered with a slight overshoot, the Dynamic Island morphs, sheets spring up, themes switch with a circle reveal.
- **Five themes:** Light, Dark (`#0B0B0D` background), "Millennium" (an XP-era homage: Tahoma, blue gradient bars, green glossy pill), "Pinstripe" (an iOS 4-era homage: pinstripes, glossy black and blue, gloss highlights) and "Wallpaper match" (frosted glass over a dusk gradient wallpaper).

**The mark must survive all five themes.** In particular it needs a flat mono version, a version that looks at home with glossy 2010-style gloss, and one that sits on frosted glass.

## Deliverables

1. **Three distinct directions**, each with a one-paragraph rationale. For example:
   - a monogram/abstract "A" or symbol
   - a symbol about proof or verification (such as a hallmark or a check-mark-meets-facet idea), executed freshly
   - a playful, character-ish or geometric mark that suits the pastel UI

   For each direction, provide:
   - **Symbol:** works at 16 px, has a clear silhouette, and holds up in mono.
   - **Wordmark** "Assay": a custom-tuned geometric/grotesk with tight tracking that pairs with Inter Display. Show lockups: horizontal, stacked, symbol only.
   - **iOS app icon** at 1024×1024 on Apple's icon grid, with no transparency and no text. Show it in iOS 18+ **Light, Dark and Tinted** variants, and also as a glossy "Pinstripe" variant and an "XP" variant for the theme-matched alternate app icons.
   - **Small-size tests:** 29, 40, 60 and 180 px, the Settings row, Spotlight and the Home Screen next to stock apps.
   - **Colour versions:** full colour, black, white, and one colour per pastel module.
2. **Glyph family:** tab glyphs for Home, Scenes, Studio, Lab and More, plus one consistent glyph each for Gild, Canvas, Cards, Alchemy, Device check, Signing, Vault, BLE and Network. The Scenes glyph matters most, because Scenes is the hero feature. Use the same stroke weight (1.9 at 24 px), round caps and joins, and a 24-px grid. They must sit on the glossy gradient tiles.
3. **Motion idea for the mark** (≤1.2 s): an app launch or empty-state animation, described frame by frame with spring parameters. For example, the symbol assembles and a "verified" tick resolves.
4. **Usage rules:** clear space (at least the symbol's x-height), minimum sizes, misuse examples, and placement on pastel, dark, glass and pinstripe backgrounds.
5. **File outputs:** SVGs (symbol, wordmark, lockups and glyphs), a 1024 PNG for each icon variant, an `AppIcon.appiconset` structure with `Contents.json`, and a one-page PDF summary.

## Hard rules

- **Original work only.** Nothing that resembles the Apple logo, SF Symbols-as-logo, Windows logos, Nugget/GoldenNugget, Erosion, StikDebug, Airship, Camlo or any existing developer-tool brand.
- **Never imply Apple endorsement.** No apple shapes and no Apple-style "official tool" cues.
- **Avoid hacker clichés:** no green terminal text, no skulls, padlocks, shields, matrix rain, cyberpunk neon or alarmist red.
- **Avoid literal gold:** no nuggets, ingots or gems unless radically reinterpreted (that direction was already rejected).
- **Accessibility:** the symbol must stay legible in mono and at 16 px, and must not rely on colour alone.
- **Be honest about availability:** don't present any name or mark as cleared. Add a note that trademark, App Store and domain checks are still needed.
- **Present, don't decide:** show the directions side by side, recommend one with reasons, and ask the user to choose.

## Tone of the brand

Calm, exact and confident, with a wink of play. Premium like Things, Linear or Halide, and as playful as the best pastel Dribbble shots. The voice says what will happen, shows progress and admits limits.
